Nationwide and Mother won Gold at 2026's IPA effectiveness awards with [Marketing and finance: Pioneering a new kind of alliance](https://ipa.co.uk/knowledge/case-studies/nationwide-marketing-and-finance-pioneering-a-new-kind-of-alliance/). Neil Charles of Sequence Analytics assisted as a contributing author to help tell and visualise their story.

> Evidence presented for the strategy’s effectiveness includes increased impact of advertising on direct sales and mental availability, faster market share growth, and reduced user acquisition costs.
