title: Forecasting
label: Service
description: Sales projections based on your current business performance, and 'what if?' scenarios that forecast the likely impact of making changes
lead: Forecasting is fraught with difficulties. It is also essential. Building a view of what will happen in the future if we make changes is why we make models in the first place.
group: Simulate
weight: 70
what_is_it: |
  A forecast can be generated in many different ways, from simple projection of a trend, to AI-driven pattern analysis, to complex and detailed modelling of the individual drivers of sales.

  All of these have a role.

  Our forecasts and ongoing performance monitoring against them are designed to answer simple questions: Where will we be if things carry on as they are? What is likely to happen if we pull an advertising or price lever? Will we hit a target with our current plan?
when_is_it_used:
  - Setting targets
  - Understanding whether current performance is enough to hit a target
  - Understanding the likely impacts of using price, promotion and advertising levers
  - Simulating market shocks, e.g. a new competitor or a major change to share of voice
why_sequence:
  - We embrace forecasting as the ultimate purpose of our modelling, but we don't pretend it's easy
  - We don't rely only on marketing mix models. MMM is not designed for forecasting
  - We track ongoing performance against forecasts, using MMM and customer propensity modelling to diagnose why performance is or is not on target
