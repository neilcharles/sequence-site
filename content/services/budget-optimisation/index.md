label: Service
description: Set budgets to hit targets, distribute a budget across media channels and time campaigns to best take advantage of seasonal sales peaks.
lead: Budget optimisation needs more than just the diminishing returns curves from a marketing mix model. It should be where all of our knowledge about advertising effectiveness comes together - MMM, benchmarks, testing and direct response - to make a more effective media plan
group: Simulate
weight: 60
what_is_it: |
  Our optimisation tools are designed to keep you in control rather than any one model driving decisions on its own. Combine effectiveness measurements based on their statistical accuracy and how much you trust them, incorporate industry benchmarks and expectations for new creatives, then build a media plan that applies the total of your knowledge.

  As well as generating a media plan, optimisation becomes a way to understand better what we know and what we're unsure about. It loops back into marketing mix modelling and media testing to set priorities for what needs to be measured more precisely.
when_is_it_used:
  - Annual and quarterly media planning
  - Answering 'what if?' questions
  - Budgeting to hit sales targets
  - Setting priorities for geo-experiments and A/B testing
why_sequence:
  - Too often, marketing mix modelling is allowed to drive the entire budget optimisation. We combine your total knowledge about advertising effectiveness, wherever those answers come from
  - Interactive planning tools generate fast answers to questions
  - We simulate using the range of possible results for each channel, not just a single best estimate number, allowing you to see which planning decisions are most likely to make a difference
