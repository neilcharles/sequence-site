what_is_it: |
  An A/B test is the closest that advertising comes to a true randomised controlled trial. We show adverts to some people and not to others, and then we track their behaviour, measuring the difference that the adverts make.

  The trick to getting A/B testing right is working out beforehand how large the test needs to be, and how long it needs to run, to generate a reliable result. We also need to ensure that the selection of targeted and non-targeted people is random, or at the very least that we're aware *how* it is not random, which in a world of algorithmically targeted advertising can be a significant challenge.

  With a good test, you learn the true incremental impact of making a change to your advertising, gaining a robust data point to feed back into marketing mix modelling and budget setting.
when_is_it_used:
