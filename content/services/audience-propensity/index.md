what_is_it: |
  Starting from your Google Analytics account, customer postcode data and store locations (just one of the three, or all of them), we match to small area geo-demographic data to produce a rich, detailed view of your customers.

  We create a highly detailed view of the UK that is aware of customer demographics, drive-times, store locations and catchment areas, overlaid with media consumption for advertising targeting.

  Customer propensity analysis becomes a powerful platform to understand your customers: Who they are now, where there are sources of growth, how to tap into them with advertising or expanded distribution and ongoing performance tracking.
